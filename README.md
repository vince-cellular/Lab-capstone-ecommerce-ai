# AI-Powered E-commerce Revenue Optimization Advisor

## Capstone Project — Round 1

### Client Context

A fictional French e-commerce SME is exploring how AI can help identify where potential revenue is being lost across the customer journey and recommend data-driven actions to improve conversion and revenue.

### Core Problem

The company wants to better understand its customer journey, identify conversion and revenue opportunities, and determine where AI could provide measurable business value.

The project focuses on the following business question:

> **Where are potential revenue opportunities being lost across the customer journey, and what data-driven actions should the company investigate to improve conversion and revenue?**

### Initial AI Solution

**AI-Powered E-commerce Revenue Optimization Advisor**

The proposed solution will analyse available e-commerce and customer-session data to identify patterns associated with non-conversion or stronger conversion and recommend appropriate actions for human review.

Initial opportunity areas include:

* Marketing & conversion optimization
* Cart / revenue recovery
* Product discovery & personalization
* Customer experience & support

These are initial hypotheses. Based on the available public dataset, the Round 1 focus will prioritize the strongest 2–3 use cases.

### Round 1 Objective

Research the e-commerce sector, evaluate public datasets, identify relevant AI use cases, and prepare a data-driven MVP pitch for teaching staff.

Round 1 deliverables include:

* Sector research and use-case analysis
* Public dataset selection and justification
* Power BI stakeholder dashboard
* Simple n8n proof of concept
* LangSmith monitoring sample
* Cost and timeline estimate
* Presentation to teaching staff
* Post-presentation `round1_decision.md`

### Data Approach

Because detailed internal client data is not available at this stage, a public e-commerce dataset is being used as a proxy to demonstrate the business problem and evaluate the proposed AI opportunities.

The current selected dataset is the **UCI Online Shoppers Purchasing Intention Dataset**.

Findings from the public dataset will not be presented as actual results from the fictional client's company.

### Important Limitations

The selected dataset does not contain advertising expenditure, detailed campaign costs, explicit cart events, individual product identities, or customer-support interactions.

Therefore, the project will not claim to measure actual advertising waste, ROAS, cart abandonment, or recovered revenue from this dataset.

The AI recommendations will be presented as **data-grounded recommendations for human review**, not as fully autonomous business decisions.

### Round 1 Decision

The final industry/use-case decision will be documented in `round1_decision.md` after the presentation to teaching staff and their feedback.

The industry or use case may be changed at that stage if the feedback indicates that a different direction would create a stronger MVP.
