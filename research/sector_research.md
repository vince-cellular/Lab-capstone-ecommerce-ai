# E-commerce Sector Research

## 1. Sector Context

The French e-commerce sector is mature, large and highly competitive.

According to FEVAD, French e-commerce generated €196.4 billion in 2025 across products and services, representing 3.2 billion online transactions. However, the average basket decreased by 3% to €62, indicating that growth is increasingly dependent on transaction volume and efficient conversion rather than simply increasing basket value.

The sector therefore provides a relevant context for an AI solution focused on identifying conversion and revenue opportunities.

The market is also highly populated. FEVAD reported approximately 153,000 active merchant websites in France in 2024.

For this capstone, we therefore model the client as a French small-to-medium-sized e-commerce company operating in a competitive digital market.

## 2. Business Challenges

Based on the sector research, several challenges are particularly relevant to our proposed client:

### Conversion efficiency

Large volumes of online traffic do not automatically translate into purchases. An e-commerce company needs to understand where customers disengage and which behaviours are associated with successful purchases.

### Customer expectations

Consumers increasingly expect relevant and personalized digital experiences. Personalization can create opportunities for stronger engagement and conversion, but it also requires appropriate use of customer data and sufficient trust.

### Marketing and acquisition efficiency

E-commerce companies need to understand which traffic sources and customer behaviours are associated with stronger commercial outcomes. However, our selected public dataset does not contain advertising expenditure, so this capstone will not claim to measure advertising waste or ROAS.

### Increasing use of AI

AI is already becoming part of the e-commerce value chain, including customer relations, marketing and personalization. This creates an opportunity for SMEs to use AI to turn existing behavioural data into actionable insights.

## 3. AI Opportunities

Our research suggests four potential AI opportunities:

### 1. AI Marketing & Conversion Optimization

Analyse customer and traffic behaviour to identify underperforming areas and recommend where marketing or targeting strategies should be investigated.

### 2. AI Cart / Revenue Recovery

Identify high-intent sessions or behaviours associated with non-conversion and recommend appropriate recovery actions.

### 3. AI Product Discovery & Personalization

Use behavioural patterns to identify opportunities for more relevant product recommendations and personalized experiences.

### 4. AI Customer Experience & Support

Use AI to classify customer requests, provide faster responses and identify cases requiring human intervention.

These four opportunities are initial hypotheses. The Round 1 pitch will ultimately focus on the 2–3 strongest use cases based on data availability, business value and MVP feasibility.

## 4. Risks and Constraints

### Data limitations

The selected public dataset is a proxy and does not represent the fictional client's actual internal data.

It does not contain advertising spend, detailed product information, customer-support interactions or actual cart recovery events.

Therefore, recommendations must not be presented as proven financial outcomes for the client.

### AI transparency

AI-generated recommendations should be based on structured analytical evidence rather than unsupported assumptions.

The proposed system should make the underlying indicators visible to business stakeholders.

### Human oversight

The AI should recommend actions rather than automatically make high-impact commercial decisions.

For example, an AI recommendation to investigate a traffic source should be reviewed by a marketing or business stakeholder before changing campaign strategy.

### Privacy and trust

Personalization and AI-driven customer experiences require appropriate handling of customer data and consideration of transparency and trust.

## 5. Relevance to an E-commerce SME

The proposed solution is particularly relevant to an SME because it focuses on a relatively narrow business question:

> Where are we potentially losing revenue across the customer journey, and what data-driven action should we investigate?

Rather than requiring a large autonomous AI system, the proposed approach can begin with existing behavioural data, a dashboard and a lightweight AI recommendation workflow.

This allows the SME to start with a small proof of concept and evaluate whether the approach creates sufficient business value before investing in a larger implementation.

## 6. Implications for Our Capstone

The sector research supports the following working hypothesis:

> An AI-powered revenue optimization advisor could help an e-commerce SME identify behavioural patterns associated with non-conversion and prioritize potential actions to improve conversion and revenue.

The public dataset will be used as a proxy to demonstrate this concept.

The Power BI dashboard will provide the analytical communication layer, while the n8n proof of concept will demonstrate how structured findings can be passed to an AI model to generate transparent, human-reviewable recommendations.

The final use-case scope will be determined after comparing the candidate use cases against the available data and Round 1 MVP requirements.
